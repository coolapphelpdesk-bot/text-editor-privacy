Privacy Policy

OmniText · last updated November 10, 2026

1. Summary

OmniText (“the app”) is a text and code editor published by TeamCoolApps. It edits, saves, and organizes text and code files.

  • OmniText does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
  • The app works entirely offline and does not request the internet permission, so nothing you open or edit ever leaves your device.
  • It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

2. Data we collect

None. OmniText has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access. It does not collect names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, usage statistics, or the contents of your files.

3. Data stored on your device

ItemWhereSent off device?
Files you choose to openFiles you select through Android’s system file picker (SAF)Never
Working copies, editing history, and auto-save dataLocal app private storageNever
App settings (theme, font size)Local app storageNever

The app does not scan or access files on its own. Files are opened only when you explicitly choose them through Android’s Storage Access Framework.

4. Permissions used

PermissionReasonWhen asked
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 / API 32 and below only)Read files handed to the app via legacy file:// linksWhen a file link is opened on older Android

The app requests no internet or network permission.

5. Network activity and third parties

OmniText makes no network requests of any kind. It includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries, and does not share data with any third party. When you use the “Share” action, Android’s standard share sheet sends the text to the app you choose; OmniText itself does not transmit anything.

6. Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It is not directed at children under 13.

7. Data retention and deletion

No data is stored off your device. Your files and editing history remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → OmniText → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

App data is held in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s built-in application sandboxing. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

9. Changes to this policy

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
